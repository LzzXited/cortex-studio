Cortex Studio is a personal tool that runs locally on its owner's computer. It creates short video clips and publishes them to the owner's own social media accounts through official APIs. It is not a public service and has no other users.

  • The owner is responsible for having the rights to the videos they publish and for following the terms and community guidelines of TikTok, Instagram and YouTube.
  • Access to each platform can be revoked at any time by disconnecting the account in the tool or in the platform's settings.
